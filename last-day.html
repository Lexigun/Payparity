Common questions

Does notice period include weekends?

This calculator counts calendar days for the notice period (as most employment contracts specify), then ensures your last day falls on a weekday. Some contracts specify "business days" — if yours does, your actual last day may be later than shown.

Is my employer required to pay out unused PTO?

It depends on your state. California, Colorado, Illinois, and a few others require PTO payout by law. Most other states leave it to company policy. Always confirm with your HR department or employee handbook — get the answer in writing before you resign.

Can my employer make me leave immediately?

Yes — they can put you on "garden leave" or ask you to leave the same day, especially for senior roles or if you're going to a competitor. In that case you're typically still paid for your notice period. Check your contract.

Should I tell my boss before HR?

Yes, almost always. Your direct manager should hear it from you first — ideally in person or on a call, never by email alone. Then follow up in writing to HR. Burning that relationship is never worth whatever you're trying to avoid.
