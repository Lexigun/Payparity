How our tools work

Every calculation on SalaryParity is built on cited public data with documented formulas. This section explains the methodology, assumptions, and limitations behind each tool.

How we adjust for cost of living, calculate take-home pay after taxes, benchmark against market data, and generate percentile rankings.

Freelance Rate Calculator
How we calculate minimum viable hourly rate from income goals, business expenses, self-employment tax, and realistic billable hours.

LLC vs Sole Proprietor
