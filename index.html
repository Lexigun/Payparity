Common questions

How accurate is the cost of living adjustment?

We use Numbeo's 2025 Cost of Living Plus Rent Index, one of the most widely cited real-time CoL databases, sourced from crowdsourced price data across thousands of contributors per city. New York City is used as the baseline (100). The adjustment gives you a directional read — for the most precise figure for your lifestyle, cross-reference with Numbeo's city comparison tool directly.

Where does the market benchmark data come from?

Market medians are calibrated from BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (May 2024), the most comprehensive government salary survey in the US, cross-referenced with Levels.fyi for tech roles and LinkedIn Salary Insights for other industries. Company size multipliers reflect real compensation premiums at different org sizes — FAANG companies pay roughly 30–40% above the market median for equivalent roles.

How is the take-home estimate calculated?

We apply 2024 federal income tax brackets, estimated state income tax for your work city, and FICA (Social Security + Medicare at 7.65%). We don't account for pre-tax deductions like 401(k) contributions or health insurance premiums — your actual take-home will typically be higher once those reduce your taxable income.

Does this work for international salaries?

Yes — select your work city and home city from the international options. Note that for international comparisons, we convert using CoL indices relative to NYC. Tax estimates for non-US cities are approximations only, as international tax systems vary enormously. The CoL adjustment is accurate; the tax estimate should be verified locally.

Is it always worth negotiating?

Yes. Over 70% of hiring managers have budget flexibility, and fewer than 40% of candidates ask. The worst realistic outcome is they say no and the offer stands. Our counter-offer email is designed to be firm but professional — it won't damage the relationship if you use it as written.
