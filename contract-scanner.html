Questions to ask before signing

    Common questions

    Is my contract text stored or shared?

    No. Your contract text is sent directly to the AI for analysis and is not stored on our servers. We don't log, save, or share the content you paste. That said, avoid pasting contracts containing sensitive personal information like Social Security numbers or bank details — redact those before scanning.

    How accurate is the AI analysis?

    The scanner is trained to identify common problematic clauses in employment and freelance contracts — non-competes, IP assignment, termination without cause, arbitration clauses, and so on. It's highly accurate for flagging known red flags but it's not a substitute for a lawyer on high-stakes agreements. Use it as a first pass, not a final word.

    What are the most common red flags in job offers?

    The most common ones: overly broad non-compete clauses, IP assignment that covers work done on your own time, mandatory arbitration that waives your right to sue, at-will termination with no severance, vague job descriptions that can be changed unilaterally, and clawback provisions on signing bonuses.

    Should I always negotiate contract terms?

    Yes — almost everything is negotiable, especially for senior roles and freelance agreements. The worst they can say is no. Focus your negotiation energy on the highest-impact clauses: IP ownership, non-compete scope and duration, termination conditions, and compensation clawbacks.
